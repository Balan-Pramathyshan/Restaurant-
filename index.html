01The experience

One room. One seating.
Twelve courses.

NOVA began with a single question: what happens when a kitchen stops chasing the clock? Everything here is cooked over live fire, in view, on a counter of black stone. Nothing leaves the pass that the room cannot see being made.

The menu is written each morning from what arrives that day. Some courses appear for a single week. The room seats twenty-four, at one long sitting, so the evening moves at the pace of the fire rather than the reservation book.

03Atmosphere

Low light, long shadows.

Charcoal plaster, brushed brass and a single line of light above each table. The room is designed to disappear so the food can arrive out of the dark.

“The best table in the city is the one you cannot quite see.”
